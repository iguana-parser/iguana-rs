- `iguana-runtime`, `iggy`, `web-ui`, `web-viewer`, `design-viewer`, `tree-widget`: MIT or Apache 2.0 (at the user's option)

The terms of `iguana` and `iguana-compiler` include an additional permission under GPL v3 section 7 that waives the GPL for code fragments embedded in generated parsers (see [iguana/LICENSE-GPL](iguana/LICENSE-GPL)). Contributions to those crates are licensed with that permission included.

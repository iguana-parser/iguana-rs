# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to iguana-rs. Setup, build, and test
workflows are covered in [docs/development.md](docs/development.md), and the
project layout in [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).

## Licensing of contributions

Unless you explicitly state otherwise, any contribution intentionally
submitted for inclusion in Iguana is licensed under `MIT OR Apache-2.0`,
without additional terms or conditions. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

If you contribute as part of your job, confirm that you have your employer's
permission when the employer owns the work.

If any part of your submission is not your own work, do not fold it silently
into the contribution. Identify its source and license in the pull request so
its compatibility and required notices can be reviewed.

## Use of AI tools

Using AI tools is fine. What matters is who stands behind the contribution:
you are responsible for the code you submit. You must understand the concepts,
code, and behavior of your change and be able to explain all three in review.
Contributions whose authors cannot do so may be closed without detailed
review.

Commits carry the submitter's name only. Do not add AI co-author or attribution
trailers; CI rejects them. Whatever tools you used, you are responsible for
having the rights to submit the result. AI output can reproduce copyrighted
material, and passing it through a tool does not remove that copyright.

Keep changes sized for human review. A large generated change may be rejected
on size alone.
